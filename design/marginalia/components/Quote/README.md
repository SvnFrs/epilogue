# Quote

A passage someone else wrote, in Literata at `quote` size, upright, with the opening quote mark hanging in the margin in entry ink.

**Provide** children (the words, without quotation marks), `source`, `work`, `ink`.

- Only for other people's words. Tyler's own lines are `Note`s.
- No left border, no box: the hanging mark is the whole frame. Leave half an em of margin for it.
