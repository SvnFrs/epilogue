# PartHeader

The top of Media or Prologue (and of their Find and Journal screens): a kicker, the part's name in `title-lg`, Journal and Find as round clay buttons, and the shelf switch.

**Provide** `title`, `kicker`, `shelf` + `onShelf` + `counts` (omit `shelf` on Find and Journal), `onFind`, `onJournal`, `onBack`.

- Kickers: the date on Media, "What hasn't happened yet" on Prologue, the part's name on its Find and Journal.
- Find answers to "/" on a keyboard. Both buttons open screens inside the same part.
