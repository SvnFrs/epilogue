# LeftOff

The bookmark card: where Tyler stopped in a work, what he was doing, the facts he'll need, and for games the keys he always forgets. A `silk` ribbon hangs from its top edge.

**Provide** `entry` (uses `entry.leftOff` and `entry.keymap`), or `leftOff` (`{where, note, when, facts: [[label, value]], short}`) and `keymap` directly; `onUpdate` shows the *Update where I left it* button, right under the note; `keysMore` replaces the *All N keys* disclosure with a line of text (use it when the full map is visible elsewhere, as on the spread).

- `where` is the position in the work's own units: Chapter 9, S2 · E5 at 23:14, Tale 24, page 112, Track 18 of 48. `note` is one or two lines in Tyler's words. `short` is the one-liner rows use.
- It goes first on an open entry's page. Never hide it behind a tab.
- Its action sits right after the note, before the facts and keys, so on an iPhone XS it is above the dock at rest. It is clay with a `lamp` glyph, because the dock's + is the screen's lamp.
- A paused entry keeps its `LeftOff` and keymap untouched; putting it back on Waiting never clears them.
- Facts are three at most (save slot, difficulty, platform…). Anything longer is a note.
