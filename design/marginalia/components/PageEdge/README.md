# PageEdge

Progress drawn as the fore-edge of a book: read pages in entry ink, unread pages as hairlines, a marker where you are; or, for things that never end, a tally of sittings.

**Provide** either `value` + `total` + `unit` ("chapter", "tale", "episode", "%"), or `sessions` (and `hours`) for endless things; optional `caption`, `note`, `size` (`md`, `sm`, `xs` with no caption), `ink`.

- Use the tally for live-service games, playlists and anything without a last page. Do not invent a total.
- Caption numbers are tabular `ui-sm`. "Chapter 9 of 16" on the left, the percentage on the right.
