# Rail

Navigation from 600px up: +, then Media with its Journal beneath, Prologue with its Journal beneath, then Find, standing in a column on the desk.

**Provide** `value` (`media`, `media-journal`, `prologue`, `prologue-journal`, `find`), `onChange(id)`, `onAdd`.

- The + does what the dock's does for the part you are in: Add entry on Media, Capture on Prologue.
- The rail is clay on `desk`, not glass: nothing scrolls under it. Labels are `ink`.
