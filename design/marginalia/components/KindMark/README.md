# KindMark

What kind of thing an entry is, as a glyph and a word: Game, Book, Manga, Anime, Film, Series, Music, Poem, Story. Nine kinds; `idea` is a plate for dreams, not a kind, and has no mark.

**Provide** `kind`.

- Kinds have no colour of their own. If something needs to look different by kind, change its shape (see `EntryCover`), not its hue.
- The kind glyphs are hand-sketched, unlike the Lucide glyphs used for actions: they are the things you collect, drawn like margin sketches.
