# Toast

A glass pill that says what just happened and offers Undo: "Noted · chapter 10".

**Provide** children (the message), `action` + `onAction`.

- It replaces confirmation dialogs for anything reversible. It sits just above the dock and leaves after about four seconds.
- It is glass, so its text, glyph and Undo are all `ink` (Undo is underlined, not lamp): over a dark cover `lamp` falls to 2.8:1.
