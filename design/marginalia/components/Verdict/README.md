# Verdict

Five clay pips and a sentence: Not for me · Passed the time · Good company · Stayed with me · Part of me now.

**Provide** `value` (0–5), `onChange` to make it editable, `ink`, `showWord`.

- Editable pips are 44px hit targets; arrow keys step, tapping the current value clears it.
- The sentence is the verdict; the pips are its shape. Never show a number, an average or half steps.
- Display-only verdicts in lists use 14px pips and can drop the word.
