# Icons

Marginalia's 39 glyphs as standalone SVG: the nine `kind-*` glyphs are Marginalia's own pen sketches; the rest are **Lucide** (v1.47.0, ISC; notice in `components/LICENSE-lucide.txt`). 24px grid, stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, one ink.

- The files are in `ink` for Paper (#2b2018). An `<img>` cannot recolour them, so on Lamplight or any coloured ground use the `Icon` component (it draws in `currentColor`) instead of these files.
- `kind-*`: game (a controller), book (with its ribbon), manga (a page of panels and a speech bubble), anime (a set with a spark), film (a clapperboard), series (the next episode behind), music (a record sliding out of its sleeve), poem (a quill), story (a castle tower). Shown with the kind's word, never coloured by kind. There are nine kinds; an idea is a dream's plate, not a kind, and has no glyph of its own.
- `state-*`: shelved (up next), paused (the ribbon inside the book), open (the bookmark), again, finished (the stamp), aside (the dog-ear). Always paired with their word.
- `part-*`: media (the library), prologue (sunrise). The dock and rail.
- `prologue-*`: dream, and the three groups inside Waiting: unsorted (the inbox), soon, someday; plus link (a task pointing at an entry) and grip (drag to sort at the desk).
- `ui-*`: now (the desk lamp: a task being done), journal, find, plus, minus, back, next, more, note, quote, close, check, undo, retry and alert (a failed save), idle (the hourglass of the one quiet question).
- Lucide files added since v1.47.0 was pinned carry a comment naming the Lucide icon they are.
